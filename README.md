Junior **Laravel developer** based in **Cairo, Egypt**. I build full-stack web apps with **PHP and Laravel**, pair them with **Inertia.js** and **Vue 3** or **React**, and style them with **Tailwind CSS**. I have shipped production systems for real clients, and I am looking for a junior or internship role on a Laravel or full-stack team.
